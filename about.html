Hi, I'm Aidan.

Originally from Arlington, Virginia, I moved to New York City in June 2025 to pursue a lifelong dream of living in Manhattan. My mom and dad being originally from Long Island, I grew up coming to New York routinely, and after 24 and a half glorious years in Virginia, I accepted an investment banking job and made the jump.

I am a Catholic, a conservative, and an extrovert. My 20+ years of Jets fandom speaks to my intense loyalty. I believe country concerts are proof that God exists and wants us to be happy. I have two sisters that rock, two parents that rule, and a battalion of friends from all walks of life that keep my screen time far higher than I'm proud.

I average 16,000 steps a day. When I sleep for seven hours, my average goes up. I batted third in little league and started for my high school football team. I tend to dominate the conversation. I watch movies in multiple sittings because the thought of having a free two hours is foreign.

I don't order steaks out because I believe I can make them better at home. My favorite vacation spot on earth is the Outer Banks, but I'll never turn down a trip to Southern California.

I was the treasurer of my fraternity, a founding member of UVA's conservative publication, a waiter at a barbecue restaurant in high school, and a bartender in Duck, North Carolina for one summer in college.

I love the Fourth of July, the Kentucky Derby, NASCAR, and Game 7s. I love Top Gun: Maverick, Whiplash, and Shawshank Redemption.

If you want to talk bank modeling, I'm the guy for you. If you want to talk U.S. politics, I know enough to be dangerous. If you want to talk NFL offensive schemes, pull up a chair.

But enough about me. Tell me about you?

Background

I attended a Catholic grade school from kindergarten through 8th grade where I walked around in slow motion. I read the morning announcements as Director of Communications and averaged 19 points a game on the CYO basketball team (no assists, obviously). I won the Spanish award in 8th grade and the March Madness bracket challenge in 5th grade. I still keep in touch with several of my classmates.

I made the decision to go to Bishop O'Connell High School afterward, where I was a tackling dummy on the football team, an all-practice squad small forward for the basketball team, and a serviceable starting catcher for the baseball team for the first two years.

By junior year, I got to start on the football team, where I squared off against Caleb Williams three times. I also achieved Eagle Scout, where we dug 18 steps into a hillside to provide pedestrians with easy access to a remote Arlington landmark. I won $2,000+ doing speech contests, had shoulder surgery, and was accepted into the University of Virginia.

At UVA I was a Vice President of Delta Upsilon for 3 years, won several intramural sports tournaments, and wrote one article that was so funny people will still text me about it from time to time. I majored in Economics because I wanted to be a banker and politics because I liked to argue about it.

My experience as treasurer of a quarter-million dollar non-profit (my fraternity) and as a campaign finance intern (fancy way of saying "one who makes candidate brochures") led me on the pursuit of investment banking jobs, where I eventually found an internship doing community bank M&A in Washington, D.C. Upon graduating, I accepted a return offer and moved to an apartment in Clarendon, working full-time as an investment banking analyst with a side hustle of being the man on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.

After one and a half years, I felt myself plateauing, and it was time to realize my dream of moving to New York City. Within a couple of months I found a job, signed a lease, and booked a train ticket 300 miles north.
