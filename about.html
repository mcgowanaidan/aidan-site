Hi, I'm Aidan.

I'm an investment banker at Cantor Fitzgerald in New York, where I work in the Financial Institutions Group covering M&A and capital markets transactions. I moved to New York about a year ago after 24 years in Washington, D.C. — a city I loved, with friends and family I still love — but New York was always the plan. It's everything I hoped it would be.

At work I take what I do seriously. Outside of it, I try to take the rest of life just as seriously. That means saying yes to things, staying in shape, dragging friends into mildly inadvisable adventures, and not letting time slip by unaccounted for. I rollerbladed from Los Angeles to San Diego. I attempted to bike the Florida Keys. I write on here when something is worth saying. The through-line is pretty simple: I don't want to waste the time I have.

I grew up in Northern Virginia with two parents who are still my biggest supporters and two younger sisters — Kendall and Ainsley — who I'm genuinely proud to call two of my closest friends. Good relationships take work and I think that's worth doing.

Background

I studied Economics and Government at the University of Virginia, which probably tells you everything you need to know about where my head was at. After school I joined Olsen Palmer in D.C., a boutique investment bank focused on community bank M&A, where I worked on fairness opinions, sell-side advisory, and a lot of deals across the southeast and midwest. In 2025 I moved to Cantor Fitzgerald in New York to help build out their FIG division. I hold my Series 79, Series 63, and SIE licenses.

Outside of finance I've been writing since college — I won a feature writing award from the Intercollegiate Studies Institute in 2022, which I still find funny given how casual this blog is. I'm an Eagle Scout, a boxing class regular, and a person who reads way more thriller novels than his professional image probably suggests.
