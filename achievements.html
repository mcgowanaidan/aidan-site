Misogi (禊)

Misogi is a Japanese purification ritual meant to cleanse the body and mind of misfortune. In Western culture, it has been interpreted as an annual challenge, in which you undertake a task so difficult that you only have a 50/50 chance of completing it – just because you can.

I had a week between jobs and a dream of roller blading from Los Angeles to San Diego for 16 months. March 2026

Key Largo to Key West — by Bike

↻ Attempted
41 Miles covered

I had the idea three weeks before to bike down the Keys once I heard it was flat. It had also been a bucket list item of mine to be the southernmost person in the United States for 10+ years. Unfortunately I came up short, as after 41 miles I realized it was more daunting than I had foreseen and I only had one day to do it. Given my exhaustion and lack of access to medics in the event of an emergency, I called it quits. But I will try again – maybe this time over two days.
